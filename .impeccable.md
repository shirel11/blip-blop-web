## Design Context

### Users
Players of the classic arcade game, now using desktop browsers and iPhone Safari. The creator specifically reports freezes and uncomfortable touch controls on iPhone 15 Pro and wants a public, shareable bilingual release.

### Brand Personality
Playful, nostalgic, direct. Preserve the game's own artwork and identity; the wrapper should get out of the way during play.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retain a dark arcade cabinet surround. Use restrained warm green accents, large legible controls, and no decorative motion competing with the game. English and Hebrew are equally supported by the browser UI.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the game visible while both thumbs operate the controls.
2. Landscape is a useful option, never a blocking orientation requirement.
3. Do not increase memory or network load for decoration.
4. Tell players what is downloading and how to recover from an error.
5. Preserve original creator credit without promoting an unrelated repository.
